01

Where it all began

The story behind Not Normal Style.

My passion for personal image started long before I knew it could become my profession.

It began when I was a little girl, watching a film in which the protagonist put on a jacket and suddenly transformed into a beautiful, confident person. I was fascinated by how one piece of clothing could change not only someone’s appearance, but also the way that person carried themselves.

From that moment, I knew what I wanted to do: help people experience that same feeling by discovering their beauty, expressing their personality and feeling confident through the clothes they wear.

02

Experience & expertise

Knowledge shaped by more than two decades in fashion.

With a degree in Fashion Design and over 20 years of experience in the fashion industry, I have built my career around understanding clothing, style, design and the relationship between fashion and personal identity.

My journey also led me to specialise in Image Consulting, bringing together technical knowledge, a creative background and a passion for helping people.

An MBA in Luxury Management adds a strategic understanding of luxury, brand value and the elevated client experience to this creative and technical foundation.

03

Why Not Normal Style?

Not made to fit in.

Not Normal Style started with a simple idea: to introduce more people to the world of personal image and show that style is about much more than following trends or wearing beautiful clothes.

I initially created the Instagram account as a space to share my knowledge, inspire people and make image consulting more accessible and understandable. But the idea grew into something bigger.

Not Normal Style represents my belief that personal style should never be about fitting into someone else’s expectations. It should be about understanding yourself, embracing what makes you different and expressing it with confidence.

Because being stylish does not mean looking like everyone else. It means looking and feeling like yourself.

04

The Not Normal philosophy

Your style. Your identity. Your rules.

My mission is to help every client feel secure, authentic, confident and genuinely happy with the person they see in the mirror.

I want the experience to go beyond discovering which colours suit you, what silhouettes flatter your body or how to build a functional wardrobe. I want you to understand yourself better, recognise your own beauty and develop a relationship with your image that feels natural and empowering.

There is no single definition of beauty, and there is no one-size-fits-all approach to personal style. My goal is not to change who you are. It is to help you discover how to express the best version of yourself.
