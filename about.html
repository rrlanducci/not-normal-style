Where it all began

The story behindNot Normal Style.

My interest in personal image began long before I knew it could become my profession.

As a child, I watched a film in which a character put on a jacket and appeared transformed. What stayed with me was not simply the change in appearance, but the confidence that came with it.

That moment introduced an idea that has remained with me ever since: clothing can help people recognise their beauty, express their personality and carry themselves differently.

02

Experience & expertise

Expertise shapedby more than twodecades in fashion.

A degree in Fashion Design, a specialisation in Image Consulting and more than 20 years in the fashion industry have shaped a practice grounded in creativity, technical knowledge and a precise understanding of style, proportion and colour.

An MBA in Luxury Management strengthened my understanding of how people, brands and services create value. It influences how I structure each consultation, communicate recommendations and design an experience that feels clear, considered and genuinely useful.

Together, these foundations support a thoughtful and highly personal approach, helping each client understand what suits them and express who they are with clarity and confidence.

03

Why Not Normal Style?

Not madeto fit in.

Not Normal Style grew from a desire to make personal image consulting feel more approachable, relevant and genuinely personal.

The Instagram space became an early way to share practical knowledge, offer visual inspiration and open conversations about style beyond trends and expectations. What began as a place to communicate those ideas gradually developed into a broader consulting practice.

The name reflects the belief at the heart of the brand: personal style should not require someone to follow a formula or disappear into somebody else’s idea of what looks right.

“Not normal” is not about standing apart for the sake of it. It is about recognising what makes each person individual and giving that identity space to be seen. Because being stylish does not mean looking like everyone else. It means looking and feeling like yourself.

04

The Not Normal philosophy

Your style.Your rules.

My role is not to impose an image or replace one identity with another. It is to help each client see themselves more clearly and make choices that feel natural, intentional and authentic.

Colour, silhouette and wardrobe are part of that process, but the experience goes further. It is about developing a more confident relationship with your image and understanding how to express who you are in daily life.

There is no single definition of beauty and no universal formula for personal style. The goal is not to change who you are. It is to make who you are more visible.
