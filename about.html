Where it all began

The story behind Not Normal Style.

My interest in personal image began long before I knew it could become my profession.

As a child, I watched a film in which a character put on a jacket and appeared transformed. What stayed with me was not simply the change in appearance, but the confidence that came with it.

That moment introduced an idea that has remained with me ever since: clothing can help people recognise their beauty, express their personality and carry themselves differently.

02

Experience & expertise

Expertise shaped bymore than two decades in fashion.

More than 20 years in fashion, a degree in Fashion Design and specialist training in Image Consulting have shaped a practice that combines creative knowledge with a precise understanding of style, proportion, colour and identity.

An MBA in Luxury Management adds a strategic perspective on brand value, refined service and the elevated client experience.

Together, this experience supports a thoughtful, highly personal approach: helping each client understand what suits them and express who they are with clarity and confidence.

03

Why Not Normal Style?

Not madeto fit in.

Not Normal Style brings together more than 20 years of experience in fashion, a background in design, specialist Image Consulting knowledge and a strategic understanding of luxury.

The Instagram space became one way to share that experience: offering practical insight, visual inspiration and a more approachable understanding of personal image.

From there, Not Normal Style developed into a personal consulting practice founded on a clear belief: style should never require someone to disappear into a trend, a formula or another person’s expectations.

“Not normal” is not about being different for the sake of it. It is about recognising what is individual, giving it space and expressing it with confidence. Because being stylish does not mean looking like everyone else. It means looking and feeling like yourself.

04

The Not Normal philosophy

Your style.Your identity.Your rules.

My role is not to impose an image or replace one identity with another. It is to help each client see themselves more clearly and make choices that feel natural, intentional and authentic.

Colour, silhouette and wardrobe are part of that process, but the experience goes further. It is about developing a more confident relationship with your image and understanding how to express who you are in daily life.

There is no single definition of beauty and no universal formula for personal style. The goal is not to change who you are. It is to make who you are more visible.
