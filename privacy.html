Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

When you contact Not Normal Style, the information you choose to share is used only to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, arrange a consultation.

This page explains what information may be collected and how it is handled.

Information we collect

Information may include your name, email address, preferred language, location and any details you choose to share in your enquiry.

Please do not include sensitive personal information such as identity documents, health information, financial information or payment details in an initial enquiry.

How information is used

Information is used only to respond to enquiries, discuss services and provide consultation-related communication.

Personal information is not sold and is not used for marketing communications.

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information where applicable.
